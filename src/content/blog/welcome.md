Welcome to the CodersMill blog. This is where Coders Mill will share news,
notes from projects, and how-tos on the things we work on: websites,
networking, back office systems, IoT, and automation.

## What to expect

- **News** about Coders Mill and the tools we build, such as the
  [Website Builder](/website-builder/).
- **Project notes** on how a piece of work came together.
- **How-tos** you can follow on your own projects.

You can follow along with the [RSS feed](/resources/blog/rss.xml), and the
[documentation](/docs/) covers the tools in detail.
